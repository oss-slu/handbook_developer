# 9. Resources

A collection of readings, videos, and tools for developers who want to go deeper. None of this is required. Your focus should be on your project and team. This exists for the curious, for those moments when you want to understand something more fully or explore a topic that came up in your work.

Pick what interests you. Ignore the rest.

## Software Craftsmanship

Short pieces on writing better code and thinking like a professional developer.

- ["The Grug Brained Developer"](https://grugbrain.dev/) - A humorous but wise take on complexity and simplicity (~10 min)
- ["Choose Boring Technology"](https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology) by Dan McKinley (~8 min)
- ["Clear is better than clever"](https://dave.cheney.net/2019/07/09/clear-is-better-than-clever) by Dave Cheney (~5 min)
- ["Write code that is easy to delete, not easy to extend"](https://programmingisterrible.com/post/139222674273/write-code-that-is-easy-to-delete-not-easy-to) (~12 min)
- ["Good Enough"](https://blog.ploeh.dk/2021/03/22/good-enough/) by Mark Seemann - On perfectionism vs. shipping (~6 min)

## Working on Teams

How to collaborate effectively with other humans (the hard part).

- ["How to Do Code Reviews Like a Human"](https://mtlynch.io/human-code-reviews-1/) by Michael Lynch (~15 min, part 1 of 2)
- ["The XY Problem"](https://xyproblem.info/) - Why people ask the wrong questions (~3 min)
- ["Blameless PostMortems"](https://www.etsy.com/codeascraft/blameless-postmortems/) from Etsy's engineering blog (~8 min)
- ["On Being A Senior Engineer"](https://www.kitchensoap.com/2012/10/25/on-being-a-senior-engineer/) by John Allspaw (~20 min, worth it)

## Open Source and Community

Understanding the ecosystem you're contributing to.

- ["How to Contribute to Open Source"](https://opensource.guide/how-to-contribute/) from GitHub's Open Source Guides (~15 min)
- ["The Open Source Contributor Funnel"](https://mikemcquaid.com/2018/08/14/the-open-source-contributor-funnel-why-people-dont-contribute-to-your-open-source-project/) by Mike McQuaid (~10 min)
- ["Readme Driven Development"](https://tom.preston-werner.com/2010/08/23/readme-driven-development.html) by Tom Preston-Werner (~5 min)
- ["Producing Open Source Software"](https://producingoss.com/) by Karl Fogel - Free book, read selectively

## Ethics and Responsibility

The "should we?" questions that come after "can we?"

- ["The Moral Economy of Tech"](https://idlewords.com/talks/sase_panel.htm) by Maciej Ceglowski (~20 min)
- ["Programming as Theory Building"](http://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/Naur.pdf) by Peter Naur - Classic paper on what programming really is (~30 min)
- ["The Ethics of Unpaid Labor and the OSS Community"](https://www.ashedryden.com/blog/the-ethics-of-unpaid-labor-and-the-oss-community) by Ashe Dryden (~12 min)
- [ACM Code of Ethics](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics) - The professional standard (~10 min)

## Career and Professional Development

Thinking about where you're going after graduation.

- ["Don't Call Yourself A Programmer"](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2011/10/28/dont-call-yourself-a-programmer/) by Patrick McKenzie (~15 min)
- ["What I Wish I Knew When I Started My Career as a Software Developer"](https://stackoverflow.blog/2022/05/30/what-i-wish-i-knew-when-i-started-my-career-as-a-software-developer/) from Stack Overflow (~8 min)
- ["Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years"](https://norvig.com/21-days.html) by Peter Norvig (~10 min)

## Videos and Talks

When you'd rather watch than read.

- ["Hammock Driven Development"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f84n5oFoZBc) by Rich Hickey (~40 min) - On thinking before coding
- ["Inventing on Principle"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PUv66718DII) by Bret Victor (~55 min) - A classic on creative tools
- ["Simple Made Easy"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SxdOUGdseq4) by Rich Hickey (~60 min) - Distinguishing simple from easy

## Podcasts

For your commute or background listening.

- [Software Engineering Daily](https://softwareengineeringdaily.com/) - Wide range of technical topics
- [Software Engineering Radio](https://se-radio.net/) - In-depth technical interviews and discussions
- [Sustain](https://podcast.sustainoss.org/) - Conversations about sustaining open source projects and communities
- [The Changelog](https://changelog.com/podcast) - Open source focused
- [The Pragmatic Engineer](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/podcast/) - Industry trends and engineering culture
