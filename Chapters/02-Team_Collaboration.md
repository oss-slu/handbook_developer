# 2. Team Collaboration

## Communication Norms
- Use the correct channel for the message (Slack, GitHub, meetings)
- Be respectful and concise
- Acknowledge messages even if you cannot act immediately

## Asking for Help: The Prepared Developer Standard
Before asking:
- Read existing documentation
- Check open issues and PRs
- Attempt a minimal solution
- Clearly state what you tried

## Team Meetings
- Review agenda beforehand
- Come prepared with updates
- Respect time boundaries

## Working with Your Tech Lead
- View feedback as growth, not criticism
- Ask questions early
- Seek mentorship proactively

## Working with Your Client
- Clients represent users
- Clarify priorities and constraints
- Ask domain questions respectfully

## Code Reviews
- Be constructive and specific
- Focus on the code, not the person
- Explain *why*, not just *what*

## Pair Programming & Collaboration
- Share context continuously
- Rotate roles when possible
- Use pairing for complex logic

## Cross-Team Collaboration
- Communicate assumptions
- Align on shared standards
- Document decisions
