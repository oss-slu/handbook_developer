# 1. Introduction and Core Principles

## Project Overview
Open Source with SLU projects are real-world, long-lived software systems developed collaboratively by student teams, faculty mentors, and external contributors.

Each project may differ in domain and technology stack, but all share a commitment to:
- Quality engineering
- Open collaboration
- Long-term sustainability

## Your Role as a Software Developer
As a developer, you are responsible not only for writing code, but also for:
- Communicating clearly
- Supporting teammates
- Maintaining project quality
- Thinking beyond the current sprint

## Guiding Principles

### Developer Empathy – *Developers are people*
Respect different skill levels, learning curves, and life constraints.

### Context is King – *Understand local reality*
Always consider project goals, constraints, and users before proposing solutions.

### Collaboration First – *We go further together*
Prefer shared understanding over individual optimization.

### Maintainability First – *Think long-term*
Write code that others can understand, test, and extend.

### Excellence – *Living our best lives*
Strive for professionalism in code, communication, and conduct.

## Time Management & Burnout Prevention
- Communicate early when overwhelmed
- Break tasks into manageable pieces
- Ask for help before deadlines
- Respect rest as part of productivity
